# Novelty check for the v3 design ("Suspect -> Judge -> Decide"), 2026-09-24

Scope: an extensive prior-art sweep for each component of the proposed v3 model, plus a
re-check of the existing claims in `LIT_REVIEW.md` against work published up to 2026-09-24.

Sources searched: web search; alphaXiv (arXiv full text); Scholar Feed (semantic search and
citation graph: forward citations of Lei & Huang 2023 and Chen & Dhingra 2023); FastTrack /
OpenAlex (which covers the ACL Anthology) including its duplication test and gap-saturation check.
Gaps in coverage: aclanthology.org and some project pages were blocked by the network, so
NLUBot101 (SemEval-2023) was judged from its abstract only, and the Open-Jev page from its search
snippet only.

## The v3 design being checked

- A. A technique-agnostic "suspicious span" head, trained on the union of all gold spans (the
  task-specific parser).
- B. Span-then-classify: each proposed span is judged per technique.
- C. The judge compares span vectors with encoded written technique definitions (Jev-style
  schema-as-data, GLiNER-style matching), which would allow unseen techniques.
- D. The paragraph label is derived from span decisions (noisy-OR, p_t = 1 - prod(1 - q_s)).
- E. Calibrated probabilities on the spans and the labels.
- Plus the existing contributions: CEM faithfulness training and testing, speed vs LLMs, and
  the Jev framing.

## Risk found for an EXISTING claim

"How to Estimate Whether You Have Found Several Needles in a Haystack: Measuring Calibration in
Multi-Label Text Classification" (Henning et al., arXiv 2609.26468, Sept 2026) shows that the
usual pooled ECE in multi-label classification is dominated by the many easy negatives and can
understate miscalibration; they propose a binning that weights positive and negative label
assignments equally. Our headline label ECE (0.008) is a pooled ECE over 19 labels with few
positives, so a reviewer who knows this paper will discount it. Action: add their binning (or
report ECE on positives and negatives separately) in `code/evaluate.py` before the full study.

Toy illustration (numbers invented for illustration, not our data): 1,000 label slots. 970 are
predicted around 0.02 and 1% of them are truly positive (gap 0.01). 30 are predicted around 0.40
and 80% of them are truly positive (gap 0.40). Pooled ECE = 0.97 x 0.01 + 0.03 x 0.40 = 0.0097 +
0.012 = 0.022: it looks calibrated even though the confident predictions are off by 40 points.

## Other new work to cite or use

- Hierarchical Prompting for propaganda (arXiv 2605.13663, 2026): hierarchical decisions help
  on low-agreement taxonomies. Together with Chen & Dhingra's annotation decision tree, this
  suggests a different "how a person reads" design: follow the annotators' decision tree
  (rational argument? emotional words? ...) instead of flat 19-way heads.
- Slavic persuasion corpus (arXiv 2607.10715, 2026; span and sentence level; Bulgarian, Polish,
  Russian) and ProBel (Arabic/English): out-of-domain and cross-lingual generalisation tests.
- MisSpans (arXiv 2601.04857, 2026): span-level false-claim identification; different task.
- PropaInsight (arXiv 2409.18997): techniques + appeals + intent with a fine-tuned Llama; generative.

## Bottom line

1. v3 as a whole (A+B+C+D+E in one pass) was not found. But A and B are taken, and C and D are
   known methods moved to a new task. On their own they give moderate, "application" novelty.
2. The defensible contribution is the combination, measured: a fast encoder whose multi-label
   persuasion decisions are DERIVED from calibrated, exact-substring evidence spans, trained with
   gold-span counterfactuals (CEM) and tested causally, compared against LLMs for speed.
   v3's noisy-OR coupling makes the "derived from evidence" part literally true, which the
   current T4 model does not do.
3. The zero-shot unseen-technique test (hold out techniques, give only their definitions) is the
   most open axis found. Nobody reports encoder zero-shot persuasion-technique detection with
   spans. It is also the riskiest: prior zero-shot attempts (CLIP, zero-shot LLMs) were weak.
4. Fix before the full study: multi-label ECE binning (2609.26468); add Lei & Huang 2023,
   ProBel 2026, UNLP 2025 two-head, MIL-BERT, Jagannatha & Yu 2020, Scheme Foils to LIT_REVIEW.
